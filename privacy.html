Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how VeeBee Code Apps ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile applications on Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the specific application you use, we may collect the following information:

  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, unique identifiers, and network information to assist with bug fixing and crash diagnostics.
  • App Usage Data: Basic aggregated analytics such as screen visits and feature interactions to help improve future updates.
  • User-Provided Information: Any feedback, emails, or support requests you voluntarily submit to us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information collected solely to:

  • Deliver, maintain, and optimize our mobile applications.
  • Analyze diagnostics, prevent errors, and squash software bugs.
  • Provide responsive user support.

3. Third-Party Services

Our applications may utilize reputable third-party services that process minimal data according to their own privacy standards:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics
  • Google AdMob (if applicable)

4. Data Retention and Account / Data Deletion

We keep collected information only as long as necessary. In full compliance with Google Play's User Data & Data Safety requirements:

How to request data deletion: You have the right to request deletion of your data or account at any time. Simply send an email to support@veebeecodes.me with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will process and confirm deletion within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please reach out and we will promptly delete it.

6. Security

We prioritize data protection and employ standard industry safeguards. However, no digital transmission or electronic storage method can guarantee 100% absolute security.

7. Contact Us

For any privacy inquiries or policy clarifications, please contact us at:
