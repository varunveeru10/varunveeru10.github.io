Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VeeBee Code Apps ("we", "us", "our"), Bengaluru, India, handles information in the Finance Calculator mobile app (package com.veebee.fincalc, "the App") and on our website https://veebeecodes.me ("the Website").

1. Summary

  • The App needs no account, login or sign-up.
  • The numbers you type into calculators, your saved calculations, goals, net worth entries, reminders and settings stay on your device. We do not receive them.
  • We do not ask for your name, PAN, Aadhaar, bank details or contacts.
  • The App shows ads (Google AdMob) unless you buy Pro. Ad providers may collect certain device and advertising data, described below.

2. Information stored on your device only

Calculator inputs and results, saved calculations, favourites, goals, net worth entries, reminders, theme and currency preferences, and your Pro status. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the App or using "Clear all" in Settings deletes it. Backup exports and PDFs you create are saved or shared only where you choose.

3. Information collected by third-party services

  • Google AdMob (ads): may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and other regions where required, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form. You can change your choice under Settings (Privacy options) and in your device's ad settings. Pro users see no ads. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play Billing: payment for Pro is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. We only learn whether a Pro purchase exists on your Google account.
  • Currency Converter: the App fetches public exchange rates from an online exchange rate service. Your amounts are not sent. Normal technical data such as your IP address is visible to that service.
  • Google Play services may process data under Google's own Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Analytics & Crash Reporting: The App does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications: to show reminders you create. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • Biometric / device lock: used only to unlock the App on your device. We never receive or store your fingerprint, face or PIN.
  • Internet access: for ads, Google Play Billing and currency rates.

5. How we use information

To run the App, show ads, process the Pro purchase through Google, fetch exchange rates, and respond to your emails. We do not sell personal data.

6. Website

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP address and browser details. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies on the Website. If you email us, we use your email and message only to reply.

7. Retention and deletion

Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear it in Settings. Because we hold no account data, there is usually nothing for us to delete. Emails you send us are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted. To request deletion of anything we hold, email contact@veebeecodes.me with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We respond within 30 days. Data held by Google (AdMob, Play) is managed through your Google account settings.

8. Children

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over and for general finance use. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect children's data. If you believe a child has contacted us, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by your device's security. We recommend a screen lock and enabling App Lock. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

10. Your rights

Under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask to access, correct or delete personal data we hold. Email us at the address below.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version. Material changes will be shown in the App or on this page.
