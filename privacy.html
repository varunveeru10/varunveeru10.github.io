Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VeeBee Code Apps ("we", "us", "our"), Bangalore, Karnataka, India, handles information in the Finance Calculator mobile app (package com.veebee.fincalc, "the App") and on our website https://veebeecodes.me ("the Website").

1. Summary

  • The App requires no account, registration, login or sign-up.
  • Your financial inputs, salary, loans, assets, goals, net worth logs and notes stay exclusively on your device in local SQLite storage. We have no servers and cannot access your data.
  • We do not collect your name, email, PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, contacts, or documents.
  • The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect your device advertising ID and technical identifiers. Upgrading to Pro permanently removes all ads.

2. Information Stored on Your Device Only

All calculations, history, bookmarks, financial goals, net worth asset/liability records, bill reminders, theme choices, and local security preferences are stored locally in an on-device SQLite database.

  • Free Tier Capacity: Stores up to 10 saved calculations, 2 financial goals, 6 net worth items, and 2 payment reminders locally.
  • Pro Lifetime Capacity: Unlocks unlimited saved calculations, goals, net worth items, and reminders.
  • Local Backup & Restore: You can export an encrypted/structured JSON backup file of your data. The backup file is saved directly to your device storage or shared via Android's native sharesheet only to destinations you select.
  • Data Deletion: Uninstalling the App or clearing app storage in your Android device settings instantly and permanently deletes all locally stored data.

3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): The free tier uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (play-services-ads) to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads (such as to unlock individual PDF exports). Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising ID (AAID), IP address, and diagnostic interaction data to deliver and measure advertisements and prevent fraud. In regions requiring consent (such as the EEA and UK), we present Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialogue. You can modify your choices at any time in the App Settings or via your Android device settings (Google > Ads). Pro users see no ads. Learn more at Google Ads Privacy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Upgrades to Pro are processed exclusively through Google Play Billing (billing-ktx). We do not collect, process, or store credit card, debit card, UPI, or banking information. We only verify the cryptographic purchase token with Google Play to activate your Pro license.
  • Currency Exchange Rates: The Currency Converter tool fetches public exchange rate tables over an HTTPS connection. Your conversion amounts and selected currencies remain strictly on your device. If offline, the tool falls back to bundled baseline rates.
  • Google Play In-App Review: We use Google's official in-app review library (review-ktx) to prompt for optional Play Store ratings without directing you outside the app.
  • Zero Cloud Analytics or Cloud Crash SDKs: The App does not integrate Google Firebase Analytics, Google Firebase Crashlytics, Sentry, Mixpanel, or any third-party telemetry tracker. If an unexpected runtime error occurs, the App creates a local text file (last_crash_report.txt) stored only on your device. This report is only shared if you explicitly choose to tap "Send Diagnostics" and email it to us via your own email client.

4. Device Permissions

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Used exclusively to load AdMob advertisements, verify Google Play Pro purchases, and refresh exchange rates. Core calculations work without internet access.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required on Android 13 (API 33) and above to display local reminder alerts for recurring bills and SIP schedules scheduled via Android WorkManager. No remote push notifications or marketing notifications are ever sent.
  • Biometric Authentication (android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC): Enables optional App Lock using your device's fingerprint or face unlock via AndroidX Biometric. Authentication occurs entirely within the Android secure hardware enclave; the App never accesses or stores your raw biometric data.
  • In-App Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): Enables the optional one-time Pro lifetime purchase via Google Play.

5. How We Use Information

Any information handled by third-party SDKs is used solely to provide core application functionality, display non-intrusive advertising in the free version, authenticate Pro entitlements, and answer direct support emails. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any personal data.

6. Website Privacy

The Website (https://veebeecodes.me) is statically hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard technical web server requests (including visitor IP addresses, user agent headers, and request timestamps) for security and operational integrity. All website assets—including fonts, CSS, scripts, and images—are self-hosted on GitHub Pages, resulting in zero external third-party network requests. We do not set cookies, run analytics scripts, or embed third-party tracking pixels on the Website. Outbound links to the Google Play Store carry standard UTM campaign parameters (e.g., source and medium) solely for install attribution within the Google Play Console; these links do not collect personally identifiable information.

7. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

Because all application data is stored solely in your device's local database, deleting the app or clearing data in Android settings deletes all your data immediately. Support emails sent to us are retained for up to 24 months for customer support records and then deleted. To request deletion of any support correspondence, email contact@veebeecodes.me with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We fulfill all verified requests within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for general audiences and adults managing personal and family finances. We do not knowingly target or collect personal information from children under the age of 18.

9. Security

Your data security relies on Android's application sandboxing and your device lock screen. For sensitive calculations and net worth tracking, we strongly recommend enabling the App Lock feature in Settings.

10. Your Rights (DPDP Act, GDPR, CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have rights regarding access, correction, and deletion of personal data. Since we do not maintain accounts or server-side databases, you exercise these rights directly on your device by managing app permissions, ad consent preferences, or clearing local storage.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory adjustments. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
