Tax Mechanics Explained

How Tax Year 2026-27 Income Tax Is Calculated

The New Tax Regime for Tax Year 2026-27 (under the Income-tax Act, 2025) incorporates the revised tax brackets and an expanded Section 87A (1961 Act numbering; renumbered under the 2025 Act) rebate. For salaried individuals, a flat ₹75,000 Standard Deduction is deducted directly from gross salary. Furthermore, taxpayers with net taxable income up to ₹12,00,000 receive a 100% tax rebate under Section 87A (1961 Act numbering; renumbered under the 2025 Act) (up to ₹60,000), resulting in zero income tax payable on gross earnings up to ₹12.75 Lakhs.

Taxable Income = Gross Salary - Standard Deduction (₹75,000)
Total Tax Payable = (Base Slab Tax - Section 87A Rebate + Surcharge) × 1.04

Step-by-Step Worked Example (Using Initial Values)

Let us calculate tax on a gross annual salary of ₹15,00,000 under Tax Year 2026-27 New Tax Regime:

  1. Gross Salary: ₹15,00,000
  2. Less Standard Deduction: ₹75,000
  3. Net Taxable Income: ₹14,25,000
  4. ₹0 to ₹4,00,000 (Nil rate): ₹0
  5. ₹4,00,001 to ₹8,00,000 (₹4,00,000 @ 5%): ₹20,000
  6. ₹8,00,001 to ₹12,00,000 (₹4,00,000 @ 10%): ₹40,000
  7. ₹12,00,001 to ₹14,25,000 (₹2,25,000 @ 15%): ₹33,750
  8. Total Base Tax before rebate: ₹20,000 + ₹40,000 + ₹33,750 = ₹93,750
  9. Section 87A (1961 Act numbering; renumbered under the 2025 Act) Rebate: Since taxable income exceeds ₹12,00,000, rebate is ₹0
  10. Marginal Relief Check: Excess income over ₹12L = ₹2,25,000. Since base tax (₹93,750) is less than excess income, no marginal relief is required.
  11. Health & Education Cess (4% of ₹93,750): ₹3,750
  12. Total Tax Payable: ₹93,750 + ₹3,750 = ₹97,500 (Effective Tax Rate: 6.50%)
