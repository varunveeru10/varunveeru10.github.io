Mathematical Mechanics

How SIP Compound Growth Is Calculated

A Systematic Investment Plan works on the principle of an annuity due, where each installment is invested at the beginning of every month and earns compound interest for the remainder of the total investment duration.

The future maturity value (\(M\)) is calculated mathematically using the compound interest formula:

M = P × [ (1 + i) × ((1 + i)^n - 1) / i ]
  • P: Fixed monthly investment amount.
  • i (Monthly Periodic Return): Annual expected return rate divided by 12 and divided by 100 (e.g. 12% p.a. = 12 / 1200 = 0.01 per month).
  • n: Total number of monthly installments paid (\(\text{Years} \times 12\)).

Step-by-Step Worked Example (Using Initial Values)

Let us calculate the wealth accumulated from a monthly SIP of ₹10,000 for 10 years (120 months) at an expected return of 12.0% p.a.:

  1. Monthly return rate: i = 12.0 / 12 / 100 = 0.01
  2. Number of monthly installments: n = 10 × 12 = 120
  3. Total capital invested: 10,000 × 120 = ₹12,00,000
  4. Compound multiplier: (1 + 0.01)^120 = (1.01)^120 ≈ 3.300387
  5. Growth factor: ((1.01)^120 - 1) / 0.01 = 2.300387 / 0.01 = 230.0387
  6. Pre-compounding factor: 10,000 × 1.01 × 230.0387 = ₹23,23,390.79
  7. Total Maturity Value (Corpus): ₹23,23,391 (rounded to nearest rupee)
  8. Net Capital Gain: ₹23,23,391 - ₹12,00,000 = ₹11,23,391
  9. Wealth Multiplier: ₹23,23,391 / ₹12,00,000 = 1.94x
