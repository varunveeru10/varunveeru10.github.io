Studio Architecture

Why Users Trust VeeBee Code Apps

We build utility apps the way we want to use them on our own phones — private, fast, and transparent.

01

On-Device Privacy

Your salary figures, net worth entries, loan plans, and goals stay strictly on your handset. We never ask for your name, phone number, PAN, Aadhaar, or contacts. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. Upgrading to Pro removes ads.

02

No Subscription Fatigue

All 64 calculators work for free. Need ad-free usage and unlimited saved items? Upgrade once with a single lifetime purchase via Google Play Billing — never a recurring monthly charge.

03

Tailored for India & Global Currencies

Native support for Indian Lakhs/Crores formatting, PPF, EPF, SSY, NPS, SCSS, Post Office schemes, and FY 2026–27 Income Tax slabs, alongside multi-currency preferences.

04

Core Calculators Work Offline

All 64 mathematical engines, charts, amortization schedules, and net worth logs run locally on your device processor without internet. An active connection is only needed for live currency rate sync, Google Play billing, and displaying ads in the free tier.

05

Instant PDF & Image Reports

Export any calculation as a clean, client-ready 1-page PDF document or branded summary card image to share over WhatsApp, email, or print. Unlimited PDF exports with Pro; single exports can be unlocked with a rewarded ad in the free version.

06

Modern Native Engineering

Built in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose with Material 3 design tokens for a responsive, modern Android experience.
